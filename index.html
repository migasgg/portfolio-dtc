How an ad gets made

My process, from a reference that already works to a clean file you can test.

01

Find the structure

I break down an ad that is already scaling: what it wants the viewer to feel and where it takes them. The structure, not the frames.

02

Model the copy

The script is rewritten for the new product from that structure. I don't translate line by line.

03

Approve face and product

The character and the product get approved before any scene is generated, so they stay consistent across every cut.

04

Prove every line

Each line of the script gets a scene that shows it, and the expression matches the line. Everything is moving video.

05

Cut for retention

Fast cuts, edit zooms, captions and sound effects, with the hook cut first and tested on its own.

06

Deliver clean

One final MP4 plus the editable project, with a fixed file name so nobody gets lost between versions.

What every cut is doing

01

Hook

The opening line and first image get their own edit. I treat them as the part of the ad that most needs testing.

02

Hold

Pacing, captions and a new visual beat every few seconds keep attention moving through the body.

03

Proof

Demos, comparisons and social proof sit where the viewer's doubt appears, not at the end.

04

Close

One clear offer and one call to action, placed when the viewer is ready to act.
